# AI Log

## Project

I Am Alive — Animal Shelter Website

## How AI was used

AI assistance was used as a learning and development aid during the HTML coursework.

### 1. Planning
AI helped break the assignment requirements into separate website pages and identify the required HTML elements.

### 2. HTML structure
AI helped draft and explain the structure of:
- `index.html`
- `animals.html`
- `help.html`
- `colophon.html`

The code was reviewed and tested by the student in VS Code.

### 3. Content organization
AI helped organize publicly available shelter information into sections, articles, tables, lists, quotations and contact information.

### 4. Form
AI helped construct an educational HTML form containing labels, fieldsets, a legend, input types, radio buttons, a checkbox, a select element, a textarea and submit/reset controls.

### 5. Images
AI helped identify a published source containing authentic photographs connected to the I Am Alive shelter. The photographs were saved locally by the student and used in the project.

### 6. Task A and Task B
AI helped structure the analysis of a real animal-welfare website and draft the explanation of how a browser processes HTML.

## Student responsibility

The student created and edited the project in VS Code, saved the files, checked the pages in a browser, checked image paths, and is responsible for reviewing the final content before submission.

## Important limitation

AI was not used to generate or replace the required authentic shelter photographs. The website uses locally saved photographs from a published source.
