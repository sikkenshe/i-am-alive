Ways to Help

Supporting an animal shelter can involve time, supplies, responsible adoption or other forms of assistance.

Volunteer

Spend Time at the Shelter

Volunteer activities may include helping with everyday shelter needs and walking dogs.

Contact the shelter before visiting to ask about current volunteer opportunities.

Useful Supplies

Published information about I Am Alive has mentioned several types of useful support.

  • Animal food
  • Groceries and meat products
  • Blankets and towels
  • Medical supplies
  • Household supplies

Responsible Support

Before You Help

  1. Choose a type of support you can provide.
  2. Contact the shelter if confirmation is needed.
  3. Follow the shelter's current instructions.
  4. Keep long-term commitments realistic.

Important Note

Information about required supplies and volunteer activities can change. Please contact the shelter before making a visit.
